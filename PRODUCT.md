# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Creators, athletes, artists, musicians, small businesses, and side-hustlers who
need one shareable link that holds everything they are. They arrive from a social
bio (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube) on a phone and decide in seconds whether this
looks worth putting their name on. They want to look professional and expensive
without hiring a designer.

## Product Purpose

TitiLinks is a premium link-in-bio platform. It exists to make an everyday
creator's single link look like a celebrity's: a styled, hosted profile page that
aggregates their links, content, products, and socials. Success means a visitor
lands, immediately reads "premium," and claims their handle. The marketing site
must sell that promise by showing a real, beautiful TitiLinks page, not by
describing features.

## Brand Personality

Aspirational, confident, warm. Voice: a luxury concierge, not a hype-bro. Three
words: premium, cinematic, inviting. It should feel like the velvet-rope version
of a link-in-bio (expensive and styled), yet the copy insists anyone can have it
("you don't have to be famous to look it"). Emotional goal: desire plus belonging.

## Anti-references

- **Generic AI SaaS**: glow orbs, noise texture, mesh gradients, gradient-filled
  buttons, Sparkles badges, floating stat chips, a dozen stacked identical
  sections, the big-number hero-metric template. This is the current site and the
  thing to kill.
- **Linktree**: playful, colorful, rounded, toy-like, mass-market. The opposite of
  premium.
- No corporate / enterprise B2B coldness; no stock "business people."

## Design Principles

1. **Show the product, don't describe it.** The hero centerpiece is a real,
   beautifully-styled TitiLinks page inside a phone, not a feature list. The page
   sells the page.
2. **Velvet rope, open door.** Look exclusive and expensive; say "anyone can have
   this." Aspiration without gatekeeping.
3. **Gold is earned, not sprayed.** One committed accent (gold) on near-black. No
   gradient text, no glow everywhere; restraint is what makes gold read as luxury.
4. **One idea per fold.** Long, deliberate, cinematic pacing. Each section carries
   a single dominant idea, never a wall of identical cards.
5. **Craft is the proof.** The marketing site is a live demo of the product's
   taste. If the landing looks templated, the product promise is a lie.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Body copy is off-white, never gold, to hold contrast on
near-black; gold is reserved for large text, CTAs, and accents. Honor
`prefers-reduced-motion` (the codebase already does for link buttons): cinematic
motion must degrade to a fully-composed static frame. Keyboard-focusable CTAs with
visible focus rings. Mobile-first, since most visitors arrive on a phone from a
social bio.
